**Groups:** This assignment will be completed in your pre-assigned groups from the beginning of the semester.

Rules:
- You will be given 10 minutes to present your solution to a part of your architecture document, as determined by your TA.
- Please prepare to speak on all scenarios, as you will not know which topic you are assigned.
- The presentation structure should closely align with what we've learnt in this course so far.
- Your TA may ask followup questions for 5 minutes. These questions are meant to help you to score additional marks.
- You are presenting to the entire tutorial - please introduce yourself before starting.

Notes:
- All presentations are conducted during tutorial times
- You must stay for the entire duration of the presentations, even if you have presented already
- You will be asked to fill in a feedback form for groups presenting, which will also be part of your individual final mark
